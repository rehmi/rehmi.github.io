## Inertial sensing

{{project_grid
    "/projects/hd-imu/" "/assets/thumbnails/inertial-sensing/hd-imu/thumb.jpg" "High-Definition IMU" "2007–2009" "A hardened, high-resolution wireless IMU for gait analysis — reconstructing each limb's stride trajectory by constrained integration of on-body inertial data."

    "/projects/haltere-imu/" "/assets/thumbnails/inertial-sensing/haltere-imu/thumb.jpg" "Haltere IMU" "2009–2010" "A bio-inspired vibrating-structure gyroscope — a driven copper-foil resonator that senses rotation the way a fly's haltere does."
